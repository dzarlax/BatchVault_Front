Image-Free Product Catalog

These directions keep the catalog scannable without assuming product photos exist.

A. Detail Cards

Card catalog without images

Preserves the current approachable card model, but replaces image area with hierarchy, package chip, recipe summary, and financial metrics.

B. Compact List

Operational product list

More rows on screen, stronger for repeated price and package maintenance. Less expressive for description and recipe context.

C. Package Board

Packaging-centered board

Makes packages first-class and image-free. Good if users think in weights or pack formats before individual product variants.
